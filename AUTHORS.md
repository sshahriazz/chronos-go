Chronos depends on open-source components — PostgreSQL, KurrentDB, OpenFGA,
Temporal, Valkey, Centrifugo, SeaweedFS, OpenBao and the Go modules listed in
`go.mod`. Each remains under its own licence, and nothing in this repository

and `go.sum`; the pinned service images are in `.env.example`.
